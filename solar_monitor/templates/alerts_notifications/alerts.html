An alert needs the problem to last for several readings in a row before it fires, and the same number of good readings before it clears. That stops a single noisy reading from raising a false alarm.

Alerts always appear in this app. Turn on a channel below to also get a message when something goes wrong. Nothing is sent until you enable a channel. Passwords and tokens are stored on this computer and never shown again.

When to send
Webhook (Slack, Discord or custom)

Posts a message to a URL. Create an incoming webhook in Slack or Discord and paste its URL here.

Telegram

Create a bot with @BotFather to get a token, message your bot once, then enter your chat ID.

Email

Uses any SMTP server. For Gmail, use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, STARTTLS, and an app password (not your normal password).
